Scope Baseline Summary

This master document synthesizes the six operational scope dimensions of the CivicConnect platform. It confirms the delivery of 100% of committed business capabilities across citizens, staff, management, and administrators, verified through 277 automated tests and audited against statutory POPIA provisions.

1. Scope Architecture Overview

The CivicConnect platform delivers an integrated municipal incident tracking lifecycle across five major architectural layers:

  • Request Intake & Deduplication: Atomic submission (TKT-xxxxxx / INC-xxxxxx) with partial index clustering and citizen auto-subscription (FR-01, FR-03).
  • Triage & Prioritization: Mathematical impact × urgency priority calculation (FR-05) with supervisor override auditing (FR-06).
  • Staff Workflow & Maintenance: Department-isolated technician queue (FR-14), unassigned ticket claim (FR-04), status transitions with history (FR-07), ongoing comments (FR-16), and physical asset linkage (FR-10).
  • Management Analytics & Privacy: Executive oversight dashboards (FR-17), overdue SLA tracking (FR-12), append-only audit trail (FR-08), and keyless POPIA staff directory projection (NFR-04).
  • Disaster Recovery & Infrastructure: Ephemeral database migrations, automated backup scripts with proven 12.27s restore RTO (NFR-03), and dual-probe health monitoring on Render cloud (NFR-09).
