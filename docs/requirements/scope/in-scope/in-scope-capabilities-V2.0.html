2.2 In-Scope Capabilities v2.0

Baseline Version: 2.0 (Milestone 4 Final Delivery) | Mapped to RTM Requirements FR-01 through FR-17

Scope Completion Status

All seventeen baseline Functional Requirements (FR-01 through FR-17) and approved Non-Functional Requirements are fully implemented and verified in the codebase. This document supersedes in-scope-capabilities-V1.0.html as the authoritative capability baseline.

2.2.1 Requester & Citizen Capabilities

  • FR-01 Submit and Categorise Service Requests Citizens and staff requesters can submit structured service requests with title, description, category selection, and location. Generates an atomic ticket (TKT-xxxxxx) and clustered incident (INC-xxxxxx). Supported by SubmissionService.cs and TicketSubmissionForm.razor.
  • FR-02 Access-Controlled Citizen Portal Login Citizens securely authenticate via email and password using Argon2id verification. Requesters are automatically redirected to their dedicated personal tracking portal at /requester/tickets.
  • FR-03 Duplicate Clustering & Subscription When multiple requesters report issues within the same category and location while an incident is active, reports automatically cluster under the existing incident and subscribe the requester for updates without cluttering staff queues.
  • FR-11 Strict Request Ownership & Privacy Isolation Requesters can view and search their own submitted tickets with full read-your-writes consistency. Foreign ticket details and private staff notes are strictly withheld (verified by 10 Equivalence Partitioning test cases and Playwright specs).
  • FR-13 Automated Citizen Notifications Subscribed requesters receive automated notifications on incident assignment, status transitions, and final resolution (including resolution summary). Verified in RtmScopeCompletionTests.cs.

2.2.2 Service Staff & Technician Capabilities

  • FR-04 Assign or Claim Request Ownership Technicians can self-claim unassigned incidents from their team queue, and supervisors can assign requests to specific technicians. Optimistic concurrency tokens prevent race conditions during simultaneous claims.
  • FR-07 Controlled Status Transitions with Audit History Requests advance through strict, validated lifecycle states (NEW → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED → CLOSED). Every transition records timestamp, actor, previous status, next status, and optional notes in incident_status_history.
  • FR-10 Asset Linkage on Resolution When resolving an incident that involves physical infrastructure or hardware, staff can select the affected asset. Database check constraints enforce that replacement resolutions mandate an asset linkage.
  • FR-14 Department-Specific Staff Request Queue Technicians access dedicated queue views filtered by their active service team membership. Queue displays active incidents sorted by priority and due date, excluding resolved/closed requests.
  • FR-15 Dynamic Search, Filtering, and Sorting Staff can search requests by keyword, filter by status, category, or team, and sort by priority or opening timestamp with sub-millisecond database index execution.
  • FR-16 Ongoing Work Logs & Comments Staff can record chronological actions and comments. Supports internal notes (visible only to staff/management) and public notes (visible to the citizen).

2.2.3 Management & Oversight Capabilities

  • FR-05 Deterministic Priority Calculation Incident priority is computed automatically from location impact (1–4) and category urgency (1–4) using the verified 16-state PriorityMatrix algorithm.
  • FR-06 Supervisor Priority Override with Mandatory Reason Supervisors and operations managers can override computed priorities with human discretion. The database enforces that override reason, actor, and timestamp must be provided together.
  • FR-08 Append-Only Audit Trail All critical entity modifications, status transitions, and priority overrides emit immutable audit records into audit_log. Database triggers strictly prevent UPDATE and DELETE operations.
  • FR-12 Overdue SLA Visibility & Due Date Recalculation Management dashboard surfaces overdue active requests where due_at < now(). Priority target changes dynamically recalculate due dates anchored to the original opening timestamp.
  • FR-17 Management Analytics & Categorical Reporting Interactive dashboard displays open status counts, resolution breakdowns by category and service team, and turnaround metrics vs SLA targets.
  • NFR-04 POPIA Staff Directory Projection View Management and reporting roles access the keyless staff_directory view, which projects staff names and active team roles while mathematically concealing passwords and personal emails.

2.2.4 Configuration, Administration & Data Privacy Capabilities

  • FR-09 Category Catalogue Management & Soft Deactivation Administrators can configure service categories, urgency levels, and default routing teams. Categories are retired via is_active = false, protecting historical incident foreign keys.
  • ASR-04 Location Hierarchy & Asset Management Recursive multi-level location hierarchy (site → floor → room) with cycle-prevention triggers and asset inventory tagging.
  • NFR-05 Automated POPIA s14 Data Retention & Disposal Scheduled retention sweeps dispose of expired idempotency keys (>7 days) and aged read notifications (>12 months), while de-identifying retired user profiles under POPIA s14(1).
  • NFR-10 Database Least Privilege Privilege Separation Catalog-level separation between runtime application DML (civic_app), ephemeral migration DDL (civic_migrate), and read-only reporting (civic_report).
  • NFR-11 Argon2id Password Storage User credentials are encrypted using industry-standard Argon2id PHC hashing ($m=19456, t=2, p=1$) with 16-byte random salts. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
